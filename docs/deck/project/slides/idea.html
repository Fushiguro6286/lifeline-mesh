The idea

Phones carry the alert themselves, phone to phone.

Person in need

Speaks, types or holds SOS. AI on the phone builds the alert.

Any phone with the app

Passes it on over Bluetooth. Keeps it if nobody is near.

Rescue team

Sees it ranked on a map, replies "help is coming" the same way.

No SIM, no tower, no internet. When any phone finds internet, it syncs to a small server, but nothing depends on that.

Lifeline Mesh · 3

Walk the three boxes left to right. Every phone with the app is a relay, like runners in a relay race. The reply travels back the same way.
