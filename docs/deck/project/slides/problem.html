The problem

When a flood or landslide hits, the network goes first.

Towers fail

Power cuts and cut cables take mobile data and calls down exactly when people need them.

People go unheard

Someone trapped on a roof can't call, can't share a location, and doesn't know if anyone heard.

Teams work blind

Rescuers arrive without knowing who needs help first, where, or which road is still open.
