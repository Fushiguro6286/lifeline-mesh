Who goes where

Each alert is paired with the team that can reach it soonest by road, counting 15 min on scene per job.

Take it

One tap sends the ack with an ETA. Every other team sees the alert is taken, so nobody doubles up.

Plan

The team's jobs in order, with minutes per leg: "First: 4 trapped, 3 min".

Drop-off

The route ends at the nearest open shelter, so people are taken somewhere safe.

Team positions travel the mesh as small beacons, so every team phone can plan with the whole picture, offline.
