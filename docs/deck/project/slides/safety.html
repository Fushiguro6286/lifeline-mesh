For the person in need

More than an SOS button.

Where to go

The nearest open shelter by walking time on the offline roads. A shelter reported full is skipped.

Find someone

Type a name to see whether they checked in safe or asked for help, and when, from what the mesh has heard.

Watch and fall alarm

Low oxygen, a racing heart or a fall: 15 s alarm, then an urgent request goes out if nobody answers.

Weather and warnings

Official NDMA warnings and a forecast check, brought in by any online phone and relayed to the rest.
